Every journey leaves a trace

A short history
of getting out there.

Travel keeps changing. The reason we go—curiosity, connection, the chance to see differently—has been with us all along.

✦

c. 2nd century BCE

Routes connect worlds

Trade paths across Asia linked distant communities, carrying more than goods: ideas, craft, language and stories travelled too.

17th–18th century

Travel becomes a study

For some, the Grand Tour made art and architecture a reason to cross borders. Seeing a place firsthand became its own education.

✈

1950s onward

The world draws closer

Commercial aviation made faraway places easier to reach, opening new possibilities for how people meet and experience the world.

✦

Today

Go your own way

With inspiration and planning at our fingertips, travel can be more personal: shaped around what matters to you, not a template.
